# Background Music

Place your royalty-free wedding instrumental here, named **`shehnai.mp3`**.

The audio player (floating button, bottom-right) loads `/audio/shehnai.mp3`.

## Where to find a good track (free, licensed for use)

- **Pixabay Music** — https://pixabay.com/music/ (search "dhol", "shehnai", "indian wedding"; free, no attribution required)
- **YouTube Audio Library** — https://www.youtube.com/audiolibrary

Pick something celebratory (dhol-based) rather than slow/soothing.

Once downloaded, rename it to `shehnai.mp3` and drop it in this folder.
